SEOPulse Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 27, 2026

Data Collection

SEOPulse does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All analysis runs locally in your browser.

What We Access

  • Current page DOM: Read temporarily to analyze SEO elements (title, meta tags, headings, images, structured data). This data never leaves your browser.
  • Tab URL: Used only to display the page URL in the audit report.

What We Do NOT Collect

  • No browsing history tracking
  • No user identification or fingerprinting
  • No analytics or telemetry
  • No cookies or local storage beyond extension defaults
  • No data sent to external servers

Third-Party Services

SEOPulse operates entirely offline and does not use any third-party services, APIs, or analytics platforms. No data is shared with any external party.

Data Storage

The extension uses chrome.storage.local only for license activation state and trial count. No user data is persisted beyond what is needed for licensing.

Permissions Justification

  • activeTab: Required to read the current page's DOM for SEO analysis when you click the extension icon.
  • scripting: Required to inject the SEO analysis script into the current page.
  • storage: Required to persist license activation state and trial usage count locally.

Your Rights

  • You can uninstall the extension at any time to remove all traces.
  • Since no personal data is collected, there is no data to delete or export.

Contact

Your data stays local. Always.
