SEOPulse Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 28, 2026

Data Collection

SEOPulse is designed with privacy as a priority. All SEO analysis runs locally in your browser. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, browsing history, or page content to our own servers.

What We Access

  • Current page DOM: Read temporarily to analyze SEO elements (title, meta tags, headings, images, structured data). This data never leaves your browser.
  • Tab URL: Used only to display the page URL in the audit report.
  • chrome.storage.local: Used to persist license activation state and trial usage count locally on your device.

What We Do NOT Collect

  • No browsing history tracking
  • No user identification or fingerprinting
  • No analytics or telemetry
  • No cookies or local storage beyond extension defaults
  • No page content or SEO data transmitted to our servers

License Verification

When you activate a Pro license, SEOPulse sends your license key to Gumroad (api.gumroad.com) for verification. This is the only external communication the extension makes. The data sent includes:

  • Your license key (for validation)
  • Product permalink (to identify the product: wjlumn)

Gumroad may return your purchase email to confirm ownership. No browsing data, page content, or SEO analysis results are ever transmitted.

Third-Party Services

SEOPulse uses Gumroad for license verification only. No other third-party services, analytics platforms, or tracking tools are used.

Gumroad's privacy practices are governed by their own Privacy Policy.

Data Storage

The extension uses chrome.storage.local only for license activation state and trial count. All data remains on your device. No data is persisted beyond what is needed for licensing.

Permissions Justification

  • activeTab: Required to read the current page's DOM for SEO analysis when you click the extension icon.
  • scripting: Required to inject the SEO analysis script into the current page.
  • storage: Required to persist license activation state and trial usage count locally.

Your Rights

  • You can uninstall the extension at any time to remove all locally stored data.
  • Since no personal data is collected by SEOPulse, there is no user data to delete, export, or request access to.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, open an issue at: GitHub Issues

Your SEO data stays local. Always.
